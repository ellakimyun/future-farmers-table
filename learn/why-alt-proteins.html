Why alternative proteins, in one tasting

A practical look at plant and fermented proteins: what they taste like, how they compare, and what they ask of the planet.

  1. 01

    Protein per plate

    3 min

    Lentils, chickpeas, hemp and tempeh all bring serious protein. Hemp and tempeh are complete proteins on their own; lentils and grains complete each other.

    • Pair legumes with grains in the same day
    • Sprouting makes lentils easier to digest
  2. 02

    Land and water

    3 min

    Plant proteins need far less land and water per gram of protein than most animal proteins. Fermentation adds flavour without adding farmland.

    • Look at protein per square metre, not per kilo
    • Seasonal legumes travel less
  3. 03

    Taste first

    3 min

    The best reason to eat differently is that it tastes good. Roast chickpeas hard, fry tempeh in thin slices, and use acid and salt generously.

    • Toast hemp seeds for nuttiness
    • Marinate tempeh for 10 minutes before frying
