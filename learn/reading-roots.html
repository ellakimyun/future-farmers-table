Reading roots: what healthy hydroponic roots look like

Roots tell you how a hydroponic plant is doing before the leaves do. Here's how to read them.

  1. 01

    White and fuzzy is good

    2 min

    Healthy roots are white to cream, firm, and covered in fine root hairs. The hairs are where water and nutrients get absorbed, so more fuzz means a hungrier, healthier plant.

    • Look through the glass in daylight
    • A faint tint from nutrients is normal
  2. 02

    Brown and slimy means low oxygen

    2 min

    Roots that turn brown, smell musty or feel slick are short of oxygen. Warm water holds less oxygen, which is why this shows up most in summer.

    • Check the water temperature: aim for 18–22°C
    • Look for a gap of air between the water and the cup
  3. 03

    Fix it: air, temperature, cleanliness

    2 min

    Give roots air (lower the water level or add a bubbler), cool the water, and clean the reservoir before replanting. Trim dead roots with clean scissors.

    • Rinse reservoirs monthly
    • Test pH: 5.8–6.2 keeps nutrients available
