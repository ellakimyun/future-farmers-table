01Key features3 points
  • Two cultures: koji for flavour, tempeh for protein.
  • A steel tray and thermometer to hold 30°C.
  • Lessons on fermentation, safety and flavour.
02What’s included5 items
  • Steel incubation tray and lid
  • Organic soybeans and rice for 2 batches
  • Koji and tempeh starter cultures
  • Probe thermometer
  • Access to 3 guided lessons
03How it works3 steps
  1. Soak, dehull and cook the soybeans.
  2. Cool them, mix in the culture and spread into the tray.
  3. Keep it warm at 30°C for 36–48 hours.
04Care and harvest~3 days

Keep finished tempeh in the fridge for 5 days, or freeze it.

05Guided learning3 lessons

A practical look at plant and fermented proteins: what they taste like, how they compare, and what they ask of the planet.
